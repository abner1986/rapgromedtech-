US Medical Billing Guidelines

State-wise, specialist-wise, and provider enrollment guidelines for the US healthcare system. Use this reference to stay compliant and reduce denials.

Disclaimer: This information is for general reference only. Billing requirements change frequently and vary by payer contract. Always consult official payer manuals, CMS guidelines, and state Medicaid websites for the most current rules.

State-wise Billing Guidelines

Each state has unique Medicaid billing rules, no-fault auto requirements, and commercial payer mandates.

State Key Billing Considerations Medicaid Website
CaliforniaMedi-Cal: strict prior authorization for high-cost imaging (CT/MRI). No-fault auto – 10-day filing deadline.dhcs.ca.gov
TexasTexas Medicaid uses TMHP. Denial reason codes require specific appeals format. Commercial: 72-hr timely filing for ER.tmhp.com
FloridaFlorida Medicaid – mandatory electronic submission. PIP (no-fault) requires 14-day billing window.fdoh.gov/Medicaid
New YorkNY Medicaid – eMedNY. Modifier 25 (E&M + procedure) requires detailed documentation. No-fault: 30-day deadline.emedny.org
IllinoisHFS Medicaid – mandatory prior auth for injectables. Commercial: 120-day timely filing.hfs.illinois.gov
PennsylvaniaPA Medicaid – PROMISe system. Diagnosis codes must be at highest specificity.dhs.pa.gov

GeorgiaGeorgia Medicaid – 7-day DRG validation for inpatient. No-fault auto: 15-day submission.mmis.georgia.gov
North CarolinaNC Medicaid NCTracks – 6-month timely filing. Commercial: 90 days.nctracks.com
MichiganMichigan Medicaid – CHAMPS system. Modifier 59 often flagged for bundled procedures.michigan.gov/mdhhs

For complete state guidelines, contact your state's Medicaid office or commercial payer provider manual.

Specialist-wise Billing Guidelines

Each specialty has unique coding, modifier, and documentation requirements.

Cardiology

  • CPT 93000-93015 (ECGs) – Modifier 26 for professional component only.
  • Stress tests (93015-93018) – bundled unless separate interpretation.
  • ICD-10: I25.10 (Atherosclerotic heart disease) requires laterality.
  • Prior authorization required for nuclear stress tests (78452).

Radiology

  • Global billing (professional + technical) vs. professional-only (Modifier 26).
  • Modifier 59 vs. 76/77 (repeat studies) – common source of denials.
  • Medicare: 95% of radiology services require 2 interpretation reports.
  • Prior auth for advanced imaging (CT/MRI/PET) – except for ER orders.

Nephrology

  • CPT 90901-90935 (dialysis) – Modifier 25 for E&M on same day.
  • Chronic kidney disease (N18.3-N18.6) – specific stage coding required.
  • Medicare: ESRD PPS – bundled payments for dialysis and related labs.
  • Modifier 59 for separate lab draws on dialysis day.

Orthopedics

  • CPT 99203-99215 (E&M) – Modifier 57 (decision for surgery) is critical.
  • Fracture codes (25500, 26600) – 90-day global surgical period.
  • ICD-10 S codes (fractures) require laterality (left/right).
  • Pre-operative imaging (X-ray, MRI) – prior authorization for non-ER.

Neurology

  • CPT 95812-95822 (EEG) – Modifier 59 for separate interpretation.
  • ICD-10 G codes (epilepsy, headache) – specify chronicity and severity.
  • Medicare: 8-min rule for EMG (95860-95870) – bill per unit.
  • Prior authorization for outpatient MRI of brain/spine.

Ophthalmology

  • CPT 92012-92014 (comprehensive exam) – Modifier 25 for separate procedure.
  • ICD-10 H codes (cataract, glaucoma) – laterality required (H26.0-H26.1).
  • Medicare: Cataract surgery global period – 90 days.
  • Prior auth for OCT (92133-92134) – commercial plans often require.

Pulmonology

  • CPT 94060 (spirometry) – Modifier 59 for separate test from E&M.
  • ICD-10 J44.9 (COPD) – requires exacerbation specifier.
  • Medicare: Bronchoscopy (31622) – prior authorization for some payers.
  • Home oxygen therapy – requires specific documentation of hypoxemia.

Dentistry

  • CDT codes (D0120-D9999) – Medical vs. dental billing differences.
  • Dental extractions – Modifier 25 for E&M if infection present.
  • ICD-10 K codes (oral diseases) – medical necessity for surgery.
  • Prior authorization for implants (D6010) – almost always required.

Provider Enrollment Guidelines

Steps to enroll with Medicare, Medicaid, and major commercial payers.

Medicare Enrollment

  • Complete CMS-855I (individual) or CMS-855A (group).
  • NPI (National Provider Identifier) – required for all claims.
  • CAQH credentialing – mandatory for most Medicare Part B.
  • Revalidation every 5 years (or per MAC discretion).
  • PTAN (Provider Transaction Access Number) issued after approval.

Medicaid Enrollment

  • Apply via each state's Medicaid portal (e.g., CA eMedNY, TX TMHP).
  • Medicaid specific credentialing – may require separate MCO (Managed Care Organization) contracts.
  • NPI and Tax ID (EIN) required.
  • Background checks and state licensing verification.
  • Re-enrollment every 3–5 years (state dependent).

Commercial Payer Enrollment

  • Credentialing via CAQH (Council for Affordable Quality Healthcare).
  • Payer-specific applications (Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Blue Cross).
  • Contracting – fee schedule negotiation and termination clauses.
  • Re-credentialing every 3 years (most plans).
  • Submit via Payer's provider portal (e.g., Availity, Navinet).

Required Documents

  • Current state medical license (copy).
  • DEA certificate (if prescribing).
  • NPI and EIN verification.
  • Professional liability insurance certificate.
  • Board certifications and education transcripts.

Typical Enrollment Timeline

StepDuration
Credentialing (CAQH)2–4 weeks

Tip: Start enrollment 4–6 months before intended start date. Delays are common.

Common Denial Reasons – US Outpatient

Avoid these top denial reasons with proper documentation and coding.

CO-16 / CO-18

Claim/service lacks information or has invalid diagnosis code(s).

CO-25 / CO-27

Procedure not covered by the patient's benefit plan / not medically necessary.

CO-22

Payment adjusted because the claim was submitted after timely filing deadline.

CO-36

Duplicate claim – same service date/procedure already paid.

CO-57

Procedure/Modifier inconsistent with other procedures performed on the same day.

PR-110

Missing prior authorization or referral.
